Common Pest Control Questions

Do I need to leave my house during treatment?

No! For general cockroach and ant gel treatments, our formulations are completely odorless and non-toxic. You can stay comfortably inside your home.

How does the anti-termite (Dimak) treatment work?

We drill small, discrete 12mm holes at 1-foot intervals along your skirting boards, inject termiticide under pressure, and seal them with matching wax/cement.

What happens if pests reappear after treatment?

All our treatments come with an ironclad service warranty. If pests return during the warranty period, we provide complete re-treatment 100% free of charge.
